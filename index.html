6 In-App Currency (Coins) & Daily Limits

Virtual Coins: Madhyamik Shokha features a virtual reward unit called "Coins." Users earn Coins by completing quizzes, maintaining study streaks, or voluntarily watching rewarded advertisements. Coins are used solely within the app to unlock educational content, PDF notes, animation stories, or profile avatars. Coins have no real-world monetary value and cannot be purchased with real money, cashed out, or transferred.

Daily Limits: To promote healthy screen habits, prevent automated abuse, and ensure fair leaderboard competition, we enforce reasonable daily limits on quiz attempts, coin earning, and rewarded ad views. We track daily usage counters tied to your user ID.

7 Avatars, Advertising & Content Protection

Avatars and Profile

Instead of uploading personal photographs (which protects student privacy), users select from pre-designed in-app Avatars. Your chosen avatar and display name are visible on the public in-app Leaderboard.

Advertising

We partner with Google AdMob to show ads that support free educational content. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Content Protection

All study materials, PDFs, videos, and animation stories are protected by technical anti-piracy measures (such as screenshot/screen-recording restrictions and encrypted streaming) to protect intellectual property.

8 Data Security & Children's Privacy

Data Security: All data transmitted between the app and Firebase servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols. While we implement industry-standard security rules, no digital platform can guarantee 100% absolute security.

Children's Privacy: Madhyamik Shokha is designed for Class 10 students (typically aged 14–16). We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided unauthorized personal data, please contact us at sibnathbairagi94@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

9 Data Retention, Account Deletion & Your Rights

Data Retention: We retain your account data and learning progress for as long as your account remains active so you don't lose your study history.

How to Delete Your Account

You have the right to permanent deletion of your account and all associated data (Name, Email, Coins, Quiz Scores). You can request account deletion directly inside the app's Profile settings or by emailing sibnathbairagi94@gmail.com with your registered email. Requests are processed within 7–14 business days.

Your Rights: You have the right to access, update, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time.
